What's new

These tools ship inside the four local workbenches. They run in your browser on the case you opened. They do not call a live partnership, merchant, vote, or bank. Catalog cards list each workbench version next to its job.

  • Share-to-hold in Partnership Breakpoint

    Solve the minimum revenue share at which a named participant still meets their floor in the open case, then apply it if you choose. The solver is local arithmetic. It does not quote a market or predict who will stay.

  • CSV roster, capacity, and notes in Partnership Breakpoint 1.4.1

    Import a participant CSV, read capacity as volume over a local ceiling (or Unbounded), and keep optional deal notes on the open case. These are files and fields you entered. They do not sync a live roster or quote capacity from a partner system.

  • Volume-to-hold, stress CSV, and studio preset in Partnership Breakpoint 1.4.2

    Solve the minimum volume at which a named participant holds, export the stress grid as CSV, duplicate the case, and start from a two-party 50/50 studio. The solver is local arithmetic. It does not quote a market or predict who will stay.

  • Roster export, file compare, and marketplace preset in Partnership Breakpoint 1.4.3

    Export or paste a participant roster, compare the open case with an imported JSON file, download the tornado chart, and start from a four-party marketplace. Duplicate names are flagged as labels you typed, not as a claim about who is the same person.

  • Waterfall SVG, compare jump, and licensor preset in Partnership Breakpoint 1.5.1

    Download the contribution waterfall as SVG, jump to compare with keyboard c, print with p, hide roster holders on screen, swap adjacent participants, and start from a Licensor and distributor case. Counts remain counts. The ranking is not a forecast of who will exit.

  • Waterfall copy, agency preset, and ledger filter in Partnership Breakpoint 1.5.2

    Copy the contribution waterfall as Markdown, jump with keyboard f w l and b, hide all-hold ledger rows on screen, and start from a Talent, agent, and platform case. Counts remain counts. The ranking is not a forecast of who will exit.

  • Residual coverage in Common Cart

    After a winning offer is chosen, leftover whole-buyer demand can be tested against the next-best other offer. Merchant export stays aggregate. This is a planning view, not checkout, inventory, or a second live order.

  • Leftover fill and overlap counts in Common Cart 1.3.1

    After the winner, leftover whole buyers can try a next offer and then a third distinct offer. Merchants see variant overlap as counts only. Quantities stay whole. This is leftover planning on the case you opened, not checkout or live inventory.

  • Offer CSV, sort, and leftover headroom in Common Cart 1.3.2

    Import merchant offers from CSV, sort buyers, filter pickup versus shipping, and read organizer leftover headroom after the winner. Merchant tables stay counts and aggregates. This is leftover planning on the case you opened, not checkout or live inventory.

  • Offer export, variant filter, and empty-offer recovery in Common Cart 1.3.3

    Export merchant offers as CSV, filter organizers by variant, sort offers, and restore example offers when the list is empty. Merchant export stays aggregates and counts. This is leftover planning on the case you opened, not checkout or live inventory.

  • Buyer CSV, leftover jump, and garden preset in Common Cart 1.4.1

    Export or paste an organizer buyer table, jump to leftover coverage, hide excluded buyers on screen, and start from a community garden bulk-seed case. Merchant views stay aggregates and counts. This is leftover planning on the case you opened, not checkout or live inventory.

  • Veto groups in The Smallest Agreement

    Mark a group whose average support must meet the approval threshold, or the higher of that threshold and the group's support floor. A veto here is a number you entered, not a legal right.

  • Package pin, locks, and notes in The Smallest Agreement 1.4.1

    Pin original, recommended, and custom packages side by side, lock a package onto every clause, and attach facilitator notes the solver ignores. A lock here is a draft choice, not a recorded vote or a legal hold.

  • Facilitator pack and group CSV in The Smallest Agreement 1.4.2

    Export a worksheet CSV, import groups, renormalize weights, lock one clause or clear every lock, and print a facilitator pack. Veto highlights are numbers you entered, not a legal right or a recorded vote.

  • Clause CSV, veto filter, and quiet-hours preset in The Smallest Agreement 1.4.3

    Import clause options from CSV, filter groups to veto-only, compare two workshop JSON files, export locks, and start from a Library Quiet Hours preset. A veto here is a number you entered, not a legal right or a recorded vote.

  • Locked-clause filter, fixture-night preset, and package copy in The Smallest Agreement 1.5.1

    Filter clause cards to current locks, paste TSV or CSV options, copy original versus recommended versus pinned labels, and start from a Sports Fixture Night preset. A veto here is a number you entered, not a legal right or a recorded vote.

  • Stall-hours preset, lock copy, and clause filters in The Smallest Agreement 1.5.2

    Filter clauses that differ from the recommendation, copy current locks, paste group TSV, and start from a Market stall hours preset. A veto here is a number you entered, not a legal right or a recorded vote.

  • Gate Gantt in Weekend Gap

    Scrub 72 hours of issuer, bank, payout, and FX windows on the synthetic calendar, with a table fallback. The drawing does not connect to a bank or a live redemption queue.

  • Queue-clear hours and Gantt compare in Weekend Gap 1.4.1

    The outcome summary reports hours to clear the synthetic queue, and a paired Gantt compares the pinned baseline with the current calendar. That compare is local to the case you opened. It does not connect to a bank or a live redemption queue.

  • Queue CSV, peak jump, and long-weekend preset in Weekend Gap 1.4.2

    Export hourly queue and gate CSVs, jump to the Gantt or peak queue hour, and start from a synthetic long-weekend Friday. Hatched Gantt rows are a local drawing. They do not connect to a bank or a live redemption queue.

  • Dashboard copy, file compare, and compressed Friday in Weekend Gap 1.4.3

    Copy dashboard numbers as Markdown, compare two scenario JSON files, and start from a synthetic compressed Friday close. Peak-queue table highlighting is a local drawing. It does not connect to a bank or a live redemption queue.

  • Gantt hour copy, payday burst, and dashboard CSV in Weekend Gap 1.5.1

    Copy the selected Gantt hour as Markdown, export a one-row dashboard CSV, jump to the first closed bank hour, and start from a Payday Friday burst. Hatched Gantt rows are a local drawing. They do not connect to a bank or a live redemption queue.

  • Peak-hour copy, holiday Monday, and gate filter in Weekend Gap 1.5.2

    Copy the peak-queue hour as Markdown, filter the Gantt to one gate, and start from a Public-holiday Monday. Hatched Gantt rows are a local drawing. They do not connect to a bank or a live redemption queue.

How it works

Review one question at a time. Inside a workbench, open its optional review panel, choose a question, and run it on the current inputs. Each table explains its assumptions and bounds. Editing inputs clears the review.

Replay without replacing your draft. Export review packet saves the inputs and calculated table. Inspect review packet recomputes them and rejects a mismatch while keeping your working case. Use the same workbench to inspect its packet. Packets may contain private names, constraints and notes; they are unsigned consistency records, not authenticated evidence.

This page is a catalog. Each Open workbench link loads one generated file from this repository. You can also open that file from disk. Node.js is only required if you want the loopback launcher or to run tests.

Standalone files

Every workbench ships standalone.html: interface, styles, and model in one document. Opening it from this launcher or by double-clicking the file does not require a network connection. The file makes no requests to Decision Labs or to anyone else.

Loopback catalog address

When this catalog is served over http from the local launcher, a control copies the loopback address. The control stays hidden if you open the page from a file.

Catalog versions

Copy versions copies the four workbench names and versions already printed on this page as Markdown. It reads this catalog list. It is not a live product version and it does not call a registry.

Catalog jobs

Copy jobs copies the four workbench names and one-sentence jobs already printed on this page as Markdown. It reads this catalog list. It is not a live product feed.

Catalog trust

Copy Trust and limits copies the heading and list items already printed in Trust and limits as Markdown. It reads this catalog page. It is not a live policy feed.

JSON export

Valid drafts can autosave in this browser. That storage is not a backup and it does not move with you. Use Export JSON to keep a portable copy, to hand a case to someone else, or to reopen the work after clearing site data. A file:// address is a path on your device, not a shareable link, so standalone mode directs sharing through that export.

Independent workbenches

The four tools do not share drafts, storage keys, or versions. A partnership case cannot open in Common Cart. Closing this catalog does not close a workbench you already opened. Treat each app as its own product that happens to live in the same repository. Keys 1 to 4 can leave a Last launched in this browser note on that card. The note is storage in this browser, not a cloud recency. Last-launched storage that is missing or unreadable is silent: the catalog shows no recency note. Press x to clear that marker in this browser and hide the notes. That clear is this-browser storage, not a cloud recency.

Trust and limits

This catalog and the four workbenches are local-first decision aids. They help you inspect a bounded case. They do not make the decision, execute a transaction, or speak for a counterparty.

  • Local-first. Pages run in your browser. The optional launcher binds loopback only. Nothing here calls a remote API or loads live market, merchant, or account data.
  • No account. There is no sign-in, cloud save, or hosted workspace. Browser storage stays on this device. Clear the site data and those drafts are gone unless you exported JSON.
  • Deterministic math. The same valid inputs produce the same outputs. The models do not sample, forecast, or assign probabilities, prices, fairness, or legitimacy.
  • Not a decision maker. A ranked shock, a pooled offer, a clause package, or a weekend queue is evidence for a conversation. People keep judgment, governing rules, and accountability.
  • Model notes live in each workbench. Formulas, units, and non-goals are documented beside the tool that uses them. This launcher does not serve those files; open the workbench when you need the exact conventions.
