About and method

A constrained operating-window model

At each hour, synthetic redemption demand enters a queue. Settlement can occur only when issuer redemption, bank settlement and Australian AUD payout windows are all open. The hourly capacity is the smallest of issuer throughput, available FX depth, payout throughput and remaining AUD reserve.

What it shows

How a closed payout chain can create queued AUD demand and lower immediately redeemable capacity even while onchain transfers continue.

What it does not show

It does not model real issuers, legal redemption rights, reserve attestations, counterparties, exchange order books, credit risk, token price discovery or operational incidents.

How to use it

Edit the synthetic fields, choose a preset, scrub the 72-hour timeline, or export a scenario. Shared links contain only the editable scenario in the URL hash.

Detailed formulas and limits are included in this standalone simulator.
