Method

Count what changes

For each combination of one option per clause, the workshop computes a weighted average of the group support scores. It selects a passing combination by lowest change cost, then fewest changed clauses, then higher approval, then option ID order.

Only combinations meeting every support floor, cost budget, and clause lock can pass. Near misses meet those constraints but miss the overall threshold.

The search is exhaustive only up to 50,000 lock-permitted combinations. Above that, it stops and tells you to reduce the choice set. An already-passing original proposal needs only one check.

Limits

Keep human judgment in the room

Scores, weights, and costs are supplied by people. The workshop makes no semantic judgment about text, fairness, representation, or scope.

Small textual edits can have large semantic effects. A passing result is a deliberation prompt, not democratic legitimacy, consent, or authority to adopt a policy.
