Alternatives

Near-miss explorer

Near misses meet every configured constraint but miss the overall threshold. Cheaper misses cost less than the recommended package. Next packages over the threshold are later passing combinations, not a fairness ranking. Gaps are in approval points and change cost only.

Method

Count what changes

For each combination of one option per clause, the workshop computes a weighted average of the group support scores. It selects a passing combination by lowest change cost, then fewest changed clauses, then higher approval, then option ID order.

Only combinations meeting every support floor, cost budget, and clause lock can pass. Near misses meet those constraints but miss the overall threshold.

The search is exhaustive only up to 50,000 lock-permitted combinations. Above that, it stops and tells you to reduce the choice set. The workshop also enumerates passing alternatives for comparison, even when the original already passes.

Limits

Keep human judgment in the room

Scores, weights, and costs are supplied by people. The workshop makes no semantic judgment about text, fairness, representation, or scope.

Small textual edits can have large semantic effects. A passing result is a deliberation prompt, not democratic legitimacy, consent, or authority to adopt a policy. This workshop does not provide legal advice.
